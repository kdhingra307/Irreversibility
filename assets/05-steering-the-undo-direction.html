Reading the two sides

Negative answers “could this just be damage?” Degradation pushes a model toward its most probable output — here, acting — and has no mechanism to make a less probable behaviour more common. So 25.0% → 45.0% at −0.2, with 86.5% still parsing, cannot be damage.

Positive answers it more directly: the model becomes more fluent. Asking falls to zero while parseable output rises to 94.5%, above the unsteered rate — there is no degradation to attribute the effect to. At the same coefficient the random vector collapses to 14.5%. Same magnitude of push, 80 points of fluency apart.

A measurement note, in the conservative direction: the classifier counts only calls wrapped in <tool_call>, so the prose refusals above score as “no call” and caution is undercounted at negative coefficients.
